Product overview

One controller for everything a hot runner needs.

The G25 is a zone-based temperature controller for injection molding hot runner systems. It heats, holds and protects every nozzle tip and manifold zone, and it gives your people the tools to set molds up fast and keep them healthy.

Each zone is regulated by the G25's own PID2 control algorithm, time-tested and proven on hot runner systems around the world. Outputs are phase-angle fired: the voltage to every heater is adjusted continuously from 0 to 240 V, instead of being switched fully on and off. The result is a very quiet temperature trace and gentler treatment of heaters.

You operate it on a Full HD touch display built into the cabinet, and on any other screen that can open a web page. Behind the interface sit a mold and process library, a material database, guided set-up wizards, analysis tools and a standard OPC UA interface to your machine.

Intelligent heating & cooling

Even Heat & Even Cool: every zone arrives together.

Nozzle tips heat in moments while a heavy manifold needs minutes. Left alone, tips sit at full temperature waiting for the manifold. Even Heat holds the fast zones back so the whole group warms up together, and Even Cool does the same on the way down.

  1. Group your zones, for example tips and manifolds, with a few taps or by drag and drop.
  2. Switch on “Use Even Heat when heating” in the Mold Wizard or in the process settings.
  3. The G25 keeps the group level. During start-up every zone stays within 11 °C (20 °F) of the coldest zone in its group, so the tips are not at setpoint for a long time waiting for the manifold.
  4. Even Cool mirrors it. On cool-down all zones of the group stay within 11 °C (20 °F) of the hottest zone while the setpoints are lowered together.
  • Gentler on the plastic
  • Less time at temperature
  • Set per group, per process

Programmable sequences

Heat-up and cool-down sequences you build once.

Define up to four stages. Each stage switches chosen groups on, or off, and decides when the next stage is allowed to begin. The sequence is stored with the process, so every mold starts the way it should.

  1. Pick the groups for each stage. Manifolds first, then tips, then gate zones: your tool, your order.
  2. Set the release condition. Move on when the zones have reached a percentage of setpoint, are within a deviation of the soak temperature, or after a fixed time.
  3. Add soak time between stages and decide whether the stage heats to setpoint at its end.
  4. Start it your way. By touch, from a machine signal (Sequence Start and Sequence Cool trigger inputs) or, for heat-up, over OPC UA from the machine control.
  • 1 to 4 stages
  • Heat-up and cool-down
  • Stored per process

Machine connectivity

OPC UA EUROMAP 82.2: speak your machine’s language.

The G25 contains an OPC UA server for the EUROMAP 82.2 hot runner interface. A machine that supports the standard can see the controller, read its zones and command it, over ordinary Ethernet and without gateways.

  1. Connect and enable. Plug the machine network into one of the two Ethernet ports and switch OPC UA on under Settings.
  2. The machine finds the hot runner device, with its identification, configuration and every zone with temperature and status.
  3. The machine takes control when you want it to. It can start the heat-up sequence, boost selected zones, request standby and adjust zone settings.
  4. Alarms and diagnostics flow back. System alarms and analog-input alarms are reported to the machine, and every command it sends appears in the G25 activity log.
Prefer wires? The G25 also offers 24 VDC remote inputs and outputs for signals such as E-stop, control inhibit, standby, sequence triggers and “OK to run”.
  • Open standard
  • Two Ethernet ports
  • Logged and secured by user

Support for the OPC UA interface on the machine side depends on your machine manufacturer.

Material database

13,800+ materials, one tap from the right setpoints.

The G25 ships with a material database of more than 13,800 grades from about 700 manufacturers. Pick the material and let the controller’s limits follow it.

  1. Search or filter. Find a grade by trade name or abbreviation, or narrow it down with the advanced search.
  2. Check the numbers. Melt temperature and the allowed minimum and maximum are shown before you commit.
  3. Apply it to all zones or a selection. The G25 uses the material’s properties to set temperature setpoints and alarm thresholds, so the process starts inside the safe window.
  4. Make it yours. Add custom materials for your own compounds, and import or export them between controllers.
  • 13,800+ grades

  • Import & export

Mold Doctor®: know your tool.

Offline diagnostics that replace guesswork with data.

  • Fault Analysis finds open, reversed or shorted thermocouples, open or shorted heaters, open fuses and ground faults.
  • Wiring Analysis clearly notifies you of mis-wired zones, along with thermocouple or heater problems.
  • Thermodynamic Analysis records zone-by-zone resistance, power, heating and cooling rates and saves them.
  • Historical comparison sets a known-good baseline against the current state of the tool.
  • Tuning Wizard calculates tuning coefficients automatically. Every result can be saved as a report.

Qualify once. Be warned early.

Process qualification captures what “good” looks like for your tool and keeps watching.

  • Qualification records reference power and heater resistance for every zone while you run good parts.
  • Plastic leak detection alarms on elevated power demand compared with the reference.
  • Heater resistance monitoring highlights degrading heaters before they fail.
  • Material protection reacts when the machine stops cycling for too long, so material does not degrade in the tool.
  • Ground fault protection and wet-heater bakeout bring damp heaters up gently on low voltage before full power is applied. You can also re-qualify just a subset of zones.
