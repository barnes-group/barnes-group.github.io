Product overview

One controller for everything a hot runner needs.

The G25 controls the temperature of every nozzle tip and manifold in your hot runner system. It heats, holds and protects them, and gives your people the tools to set molds up quickly and keep them in good shape.

Behind the precision is the G25's own PID2 control, proven on hot runner systems around the world. Instead of simply switching heaters fully on and off, the G25 adjusts the power to each heater smoothly, from zero to full. The result is a steady temperature and gentler treatment of your heaters.

You operate it on the Full HD touch screen built into the cabinet, and on any other screen that can open a web page. Behind the screen you will find a library of molds and processes, a plastics database, guided set-up, analysis tools and a standard connection to your injection molding machine.

Intelligent heating & cooling

Even Heat & Even Cool: every zone arrives together.

Nozzle tips heat in moments while a heavy manifold needs minutes. Left alone, tips sit at full temperature waiting for the manifold. Even Heat holds the fast zones back so the whole group warms up together, and Even Cool does the same on the way down.

  1. Group your zones, for example tips and manifolds, with a few taps or by drag and drop.
  2. Switch on “Use Even Heat when heating” in the Mold Wizard or in the process settings.
  3. The G25 keeps the group level. During start-up every zone stays within 11 °C (20 °F) of the coldest zone in its group, so the tips are not at setpoint for a long time waiting for the manifold.
  4. Even Cool mirrors it. On cool-down all zones of the group stay within 11 °C (20 °F) of the hottest zone while the setpoints are lowered together.
  • Gentler on the plastic
  • Less time at temperature
  • Set per group, per process

Programmable sequences

Heat-up and cool-down sequences you build once.

Decide the order in which groups of zones heat up or cool down, in up to four stages. The sequence is saved with the process, so every mold starts the way it should.

  1. Choose the groups for each stage. Manifolds first, then tips, then gate zones: your tool, your order.
  2. Choose when the next stage may start. When the zones have reached a set share of their target temperature, are close to a hold temperature, or after a fixed time.
  3. Add a hold time (“soak”) between stages, and decide whether a stage heats fully to target at its end.
  4. Start it your way. By touch, from a signal of your machine, or, for heating, over OPC UA from the machine control.
  • 1 to 4 stages
  • Heat-up and cool-down
  • Stored per process
Machine connectivity

OPC UA EUROMAP 82.2: speak your machine’s language.

The G25 has a built-in OPC UA server for the EUROMAP 82.2 hot runner interface. A machine that supports the standard can see the controller, read every zone and command it over ordinary Ethernet, with no extra gateway.

  1. Connect and switch on. Plug the machine network into one of the two Ethernet ports and enable OPC UA in the settings.
  2. The machine finds the hot runner, with its identification, its configuration and every zone with temperature and status.
  3. The machine can take control. It can start the heat-up sequence, boost selected zones, request standby and adjust zone settings.
  4. Alarms flow back. System alarms and alarms from extra measurements are reported to the machine, and every command it sends is recorded in the G25 activity log.
Prefer wires? The G25 also has 24 V inputs and outputs for signals such as emergency stop, standby, start of heating and “OK to run”.
  • Open standard
  • Two Ethernet ports
  • Every command logged

Whether your machine supports this interface depends on the machine manufacturer.

Modern user interface

A modern touch screen that feels like today’s software.

The G25 screen is designed for touch first. It is quick and clear, shows every zone live, and adapts to upright and wide screens.

  1. See your tool the way you need to. Switch between zone tiles, tables, bar and line charts and the Tool Graph with a single tap.
  2. Change what you mean to change. Select one zone, a group or all zones and enter temperatures on a purpose-built keypad, even when the zones have different targets.
  3. Group zones by drag and drop and give them names that make sense to you.
  4. Work in your language. The screen and its on-screen keyboards support 18 languages, and five user levels show each person only what they need.
  • Touch-first
  • Portrait & landscape
  • Adjustable screen brightness
  • 18 languages

Material database

13,800+ plastics, one tap from the right settings.

The G25 comes with a database of more than 13,800 plastic grades from about 700 manufacturers. Pick your material and let the controller’s limits follow it.

  1. Search or filter. Find a grade by name or abbreviation, or narrow it down with the advanced search.
  2. Check the numbers. The melt temperature and the allowed minimum and maximum are shown before you commit.
  3. Apply it to all zones or a selection. The G25 sets temperatures and alarm limits from the material, so your process starts inside the safe window.
  4. Make it yours. Add your own compounds, and import or export materials between controllers.
  • 13,800+ grades
  • Your own materials
  • Import & export

From the first temperature setpoint to the last service report. Choose an area and tap any screenshot to enlarge it.

Monitor and control every zone.

Live temperatures, targets and alarms for every zone, for every operator.

  • Live zone tiles show the actual temperature, target, heater power and current at a glance.
  • Many views of the same tool: tiles, tables, bar and line charts, and the Tool Graph.
  • Change temperatures for one zone, a group or everything, even when the targets differ.
  • Boost warms zones briefly to clear cold plastic at start-up, Standby protects the material while the machine is idle, Trim shifts a temperature permanently.
  • Clear alarms: a critical overtemperature turns the header red and names the zone.

From a new mold to a running process in minutes.

Mold and process management with reusable definitions, guided by wizards.

  • Mold Wizard guides you from the basic mold details through automatic zone detection and a wiring check to heating, cooling and qualification.
  • Quick Mold gets a simple tool running with just a name and a temperature.
  • Zone types and groups: tip, manifold or monitor. Rename and regroup by drag and drop.
  • Fine-tuning per zone for special cases.
  • Reusable molds and processes: copy, edit, export and import, including molds from earlier Gammaflux controllers.

Mold Doctor®: know your tool.

Offline diagnostics that replace guesswork with data.

  • Fault Analysis finds open, reversed or shorted thermocouples, open or shorted heaters, open fuses and ground faults.
  • Wiring Analysis clearly notifies you of mis-wired zones, along with thermocouple or heater problems.
  • Thermodynamic Analysis records resistance, power, and heating and cooling rates zone by zone, and saves them.
  • Historical comparison sets a known-good result against the current state of the tool.
  • Tuning Wizard works out the control settings for you. Every result can be saved as a report.

Qualify once. Be warned early.

Process qualification captures what “good” looks like for your tool and keeps watching.

  • Qualification records power and heater resistance for every zone while you run good parts.
  • Plastic leak detection raises an alarm when a zone needs more power than it should.
  • Heater monitoring shows ageing heaters before they fail.
  • Material protection steps in when the machine stops cycling for too long, so plastic does not degrade in the tool.
  • Heater protection: damp heaters are dried out gently on low power before full power is applied, and ground faults are detected. You can also re-qualify just some of the zones.

Your tool, on screen.

The Tool Graph links the controller to the documentation of your mold.

  • Add a picture of the tool, for example a photograph of the manifold, as a named view.
  • Place the zones on it, where they physically are, and move them until they match.
  • Keep several views per mold, each with its own picture and zone placement.
  • Stored with the mold, so the right pictures are there whenever that mold is activated.

See beyond temperature.

Analog channels and remote I/O bring the rest of the process into the same screen.

  • Extra measurement inputs capture values such as cooling-water flow, shown live as bar or line charts.
  • For every channel: name, unit, range, calibration and low and high alarms with selectable actions.
  • Extra input and output modules add signals wherever you need them, from machine inputs to alarm outputs.
  • Fully integrated: alarms from these measurements show up in the alarm list and can be reported to your machine.

Data you can hand to anyone.

Reports, history and exports for quality, maintenance and customers.

  • Process reports include temperatures, alarm limits, heating and cooling steps and qualification results.
  • Analysis reports are saved from Mold Doctor® and can be reviewed and compared later.
  • History: record temperatures over time and show them in a table you configure.
  • Activity log records who changed what and when, with filters and export.
  • Export to USB as PDF or CSV, plus complete database backup and restore.
