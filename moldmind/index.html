Product overview

Know where your molds are and what they have done.

The moldMIND X is a small device that is built into the mold. It counts every cycle and sends its data to moldHUB, a web application where you can see all your molds at a glance.

The device counts the cycles and works out the average cycle time. It also measures the temperature, notices when it is taken out of the mold, and reports its approximate location from the mobile network.

In moldHUB you link every device to its mold. From then on you see cycles, cycle time, utilization, temperature and location of every mold, get an event when something needs attention, and keep the maintenance of your molds under control.

The moldMIND X is part of the mold: it has no display and needs a mold with a pocket for it. moldHUB is a monitoring tool. It shows what your molds are doing, but it does not control the machine.

Features

What you get with moldMIND X and moldHUB.

Six things that change how you keep track of your molds. Pick one to see how it works. Every screen on this page is a real screenshot from moldHUB.

Cycle counting

Every cycle counted, at the mold.

A pin on the moldMIND X is moved by the mold at every cycle. The device counts the cycles and works out the average cycle time and close time.

  1. Mount it in the mold. Put the device into the pocket of the mold and fasten it with two screws.
  2. Close the mold once. A blue LED flashes briefly. This confirms that the cycle pin is moved correctly.
  3. Link it to the mold in moldHUB. Use NFC, the serial number or a QR code.
  4. Read the data. The device sends the cycle count, cycle time, temperature and location to moldHUB, by default once per hour.
  5. No network or low battery? Hold your smartphone to the NFC logo on the device. The phone reads the cycle count and passes it on to moldHUB.
  • No wiring to the machine
  • Counts without network
  • Battery lasts several months
Fleet overview

All your molds in one overview.

The moldHUB overview shows what your molds have produced on the days you choose: parts, cycles, cycle time and utilization.

  1. Choose the days you want to look at with the date picker.
  2. See the numbers as cards and charts: parts produced, cycles, cycle time and utilization.
  3. Filter your molds and switch between a list and tiles. Open a mold to see all its details.
  4. Find every mold on the map. The map shows where your molds are.
  • Any date range
  • List or tile view
  • Map of all molds
Maintenance & events

Maintenance on time, not too late.

Decide how many cycles a mold may run before it needs maintenance. moldHUB keeps count and tells you when it is time.

  1. Enter the maintenance cycles for the mold.
  2. Follow the counter. The maintenance counter is shown against your limit in the mold list and on the mold page.
  3. Watch the events. moldHUB records events for temperature, battery, invalid cycle counts and maintenance. You can add comments to them.
  4. Get notified and close the loop. Events show up in the notification bell and can be sent by e-mail. When the work is finished, mark the maintenance as done.
  • Maintenance counter
  • Alerts in the app and by e-mail
  • Comments on events
Location & history

Where is my mold, and what has it been through?

Every device reports its approximate location together with its battery level, temperature and cycles.

  1. See it on the map. The device page shows the last place the device reported from and when it was last active.
  2. Follow the history. Charts show cycles, battery and temperature over the days you choose.
  3. Know if it is removed. The device notices when it is taken out of the mold and records the time.
Good to know: the location comes from the mobile network cell the device is connected to, so it shows the area where the mold is, not the exact spot.
  • Map with last position
  • Battery and temperature history
  • Removal recorded
On your phone

Install moldHUB as an app.

moldHUB runs in the browser of your phone, tablet or PC. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with nothing to download from a store.

  1. Open moldhub.io in your browser and sign in.
  2. Add it to your home screen or install it from the browser menu.
  3. Open it like any other app. You get the same overview, molds and devices as on a PC, fitted to the screen of your phone.

Access & security

The right people see the right molds.

moldHUB has four user roles. A molder sees only the molds that were assigned to them, while administrators manage users, molds and devices.

  1. Choose a role for every user: Admin, User, Simple User or Molder.
  2. Assign molds to your molders so that they see exactly what they work with.
  3. Protect every login with two-factor authentication, by authenticator app or by an e-mail code.
  4. Make it yours. Every customer account can have its own theme and logo, and you can choose °C or °F.

The device

Small enough for a pocket in the mold.

The moldMIND X is installed in a pocket of the mold. It also fits the existing pockets of classic mechanical cycle counters, so you can retrofit molds you already have. It needs no cables to the machine and no display: moldHUB is where you see the results.

What you need to know before you mount it.

The pocket must be made of steel, because the dismantle detection needs contact with the steel of the mold. The cycle pin must be moved by the moving part of the mold.

  1. Make the pocket 48 × 38 × 26 mm in the mold steel.
  2. Fasten the device with two M4 × 50 screws at 3 Nm. The housing is made of nylon, so do not tighten more.
  3. Close the mold once and check that the blue LED flashes.

What the lights tell you

  • Green LED flashing: searching for the mobile network
  • Green LED on: connected to the mobile network
  • Blue LED flashing: connecting to moldHUB
  • Blue LED on: connected to moldHUB
  • Red LED on: sending data
  • Short blue flash: a cycle was counted

No light means normal operation. The device sleeps to save the battery.

Charge by USB-C

Connect a USB-C cable and a charger of up to 1 A. A charge typically takes less than 30 minutes.

Read it by NFC

Hold your smartphone to the NFC logo on the device. The phone reads the cycle count and passes it on to moldHUB, so the data arrives even when the device has no mobile reception or the battery is low. The value on the device is updated at least once per hour.

Update over USB-C

New firmware comes as a file from Männer and is copied to the device over the USB-C port.
